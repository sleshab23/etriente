How to explore

Etriente is a little town you walk around. Every building is a part of the blog.
WASD Walk around
↑←↓→ Also walk around
Shift Run
E Enter / interact
M Town map
C Change camera zoom
Esc Close / pause
🖱️ drag Look around
📱 Joystick + A button on touch screens

Five buildings hold the blog: the Bookstore (reading & writing), the Art Studio (animation & design), the Record Store (music), the Library (learning) and the Little Home (diary & memories). The stone map board in the middle of the square opens the town map.
